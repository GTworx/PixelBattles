# Charles d'Albret, Constable of France

## Summary

Constable of France and nominal commander of the French army at Agincourt, killed in the battle.

## Career

Held the highest military office in France. He opposed immediate battle (as did Boucicaut) and favoured starving the English, but the younger nobles forced an attack. He led the vanguard.

## Command style

A cautious professional who was overruled by a confident, factional aristocracy; with the king absent through illness, no single commander could impose the plan.
