# Edward of Norwich, Duke of York

## Summary

Cousin of Henry V who commanded the English vanguard on the right wing at Agincourt and was killed.

## Career

A cousin of the king and a senior commander in his army.

## Command style

Led the English right, the vanguard battle of men-at-arms and the archers beside it.
