# Friedrich Paulus

## Summary

Commander of the German 6th Army, who surrendered at Stalingrad.

## Career

Commander of the 6th Army, which attacked Stalingrad in August 1942. Encircled in November, he asked to break out but obeyed Hitler's order to hold, and did not attempt a breakout during Winter Storm (12-23 December). Promoted to field marshal on 30 January 1943, he was captured on 31 January and the northern pocket surrendered on 2 February.

## Command style

Cautious staff officer, dependent on orders from Hitler and on the Luftwaffe airlift, which never reached the needed supply level.
