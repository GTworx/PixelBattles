# Alp Arslan

Seljuk sultan who destroyed the army of Romanos IV at [[manzikert]].

## Summary

Son of Chaghri Beg, malik of Khorasan from 1060 and sultan from 1063. He campaigned against the Kara-Khanids, Ghaznavids and the Georgian and Armenian kingdoms.

## Career

At Manzikert he lured the Byzantine centre across the plain, then encircled and routed it and captured the emperor. He reportedly offered peace terms including territorial concessions and a ransom, and treated Romanos courteously once identified. In 1072, campaigning in Transoxiana, he was stabbed by a captured fortress commander at Barzam and died four days later; he was buried at Merv and his son Malik-Shah succeeded him.

## Command style

Led in person, favoured deception and ambush, and kept a qadi with him in battle. He exploited the mobility of [[horse-archers]] and the [[feigned-retreat]].
