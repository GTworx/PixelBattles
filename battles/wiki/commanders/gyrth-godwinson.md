# Gyrth Godwinson

Brother of [[harold-ii-godwinson]]; killed at [[hastings]].

## Summary

Gyrth fought beside the king at Hastings. Accounts suggest he and Leofwine may have led the English pursuit of the fleeing Bretons, and both died. The sources consulted mention him only as one of the brothers killed, so biographical dates and offices are not established here; the scenario shows his contingent (with Leofwine's) destroyed in the pursuit around midday.
