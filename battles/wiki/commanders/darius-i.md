# Darius I

## Summary

King of Persia 522-486 BCE who ordered the 490 BCE expedition that ended at [[marathon]] after Athens aided the Ionian Revolt.

## Career

He reorganised the empire into satrapies, built the Royal Road, subdued Thrace and Macedon, crushed the Ionian Revolt (493), and sent first Mardonius (492) and then [[datis]] and [[artaphernes]] (490) against Greece. He died in 486 BCE while preparing a larger campaign, which his son [[xerxes-i]] carried out.

## Command style

An administrator more than a field general; he commanded by delegation.
