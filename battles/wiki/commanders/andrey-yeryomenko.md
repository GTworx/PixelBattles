# Andrey Yeryomenko

## Summary

Commander of the Southeastern (later Stalingrad) Front from 1 August 1942, responsible for the southern part of the defence and the southern pincer of Uranus.

## Career

Appointed by Stalin on 1 August 1942 to command the Southeastern Front, created 4 August; on 28 September he took over the reorganized Stalingrad Front. Its armies included the 62nd and 64th Armies; its 51st and 57th Armies struck the Romanian Fourth Army on 20 November.

## Command style

Forceful and politically close to Stalin and Khrushchev, who served on his military council. Judged by the speed of the southern attack: the southern force met the northern at Kalach on 23 November.
