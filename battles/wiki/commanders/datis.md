# Datis

## Summary

Median admiral and the senior commander of Darius I's 490 BCE expedition against Athens and Eretria; he led the Persian forces at [[marathon]] with [[artaphernes]].

## Career

Datis took the Cyclades, spared Delos, sacked Eretria and landed at Marathon on the advice of the exile Hippias. After the defeat he sailed back to Asia, bringing Eretrian captives and the sacred statue of Apollo he had recovered from Boeotia.

## Command style

Cautious and methodical: a seaborne operation, a defensive beach camp, and a battle accepted only after five days. Herodotus places him in the Persian centre.
