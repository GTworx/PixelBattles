# Giovanni Giustiniani Longo

## Summary

Genoese soldier who commanded the land-wall defence and whose wounding on 29 May helped break the defence.

## Career

Arrived in January 1453 with about 700 men and was given overall command of the land walls. Held the Charisian Gate and then joined Constantine at the Mesoteichion stockade. Given the rank of protostrator, he was severely wounded in the final assault, left the wall, and died on 1 June 1453, being buried on Chios. Venetian sources accused him of feigning the wound; Genoa denied it.

## Command style

A specialist in defending fortified places who organised repairs of the damaged walls and the stockade.
