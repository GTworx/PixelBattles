# Lucius Aemilius Paullus

## Summary

Roman consul in 216 BCE who commanded the right wing and shared command with [[gaius-terentius-varro]] at [[cannae]]. He was killed in the battle.

## Career

Consul in 216 BCE. He opposed fighting Hannibal on the open plain at Cannae but was overruled by his colleague and the Senate's wish for battle. In Livy's account he was wounded early by a sling stone, refused the tribune Lentulus's offer of a horse and told him to warn the Senate, and was killed by fugitives who did not recognise him; Livy puts the blame on Varro.

## Command style

Cautious and methodical, closer to the Fabian approach than his colleague. On 1 August he declined [[hannibal-barca]]'s offer of battle, and he placed a third of the army in a smaller camp across the river; when the Romans formed on 2 August he led the right, on the Aufidus, with the citizen cavalry.
