# Theodore Alyates

Commander of the Byzantine right wing at [[manzikert]].

## Summary

Alyates led the right wing, which misread Romanos's order to withdraw at dusk and was the first part of the army to break under the Seljuk counter-attack. Further biography is not covered by the pages consulted.
