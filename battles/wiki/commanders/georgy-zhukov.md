# Georgy Zhukov

## Summary

Marshal of the Soviet Union and Stavka representative who, with Aleksandr Vasilevsky, planned the Stalingrad counteroffensive.

## Career

Senior Stavka commander and deputy supreme commander in 1942. At Stalingrad he coordinated Stavka planning (one source has him taking overall command of the Stalingrad front on 29 August before moving on) and planned Uranus, which Vasilevsky then carried out.

## Command style

Methodical, ruthless and fond of mass: fronts assembled secretly on the flanks, heavy artillery preparation, and tank corps for deep exploitation. Operation Mars (against Army Group Centre) was launched at the same time as [[operation-uranus]].
