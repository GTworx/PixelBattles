# Miltiades

## Summary

Athenian strategos who guided the Greek plan at [[marathon]] (490 BCE). He argued for attacking the Persians at once and, by Herodotus's account, persuaded the polemarch [[callimachus]] to cast the deciding vote.

## Career

Born into the Philaid family, Miltiades ruled the Thracian Chersonese as a vassal of Darius, joined the Ionian Revolt and fled to Athens when it failed. He had served in Persia's army and knew its tactics. After Marathon he led a fleet against Paros in 489, failed, was wounded and was fined by the Athenians. He died soon after of gangrene.

## Command style

Aggressive and well informed about the Persians. At Marathon he is credited with the weakened centre and strong wings, and with the pivot into a [[double-envelopment]], although how much of this is hindsight is debated.
