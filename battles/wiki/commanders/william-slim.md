# William Slim

## Summary

British general who led Burma Corps in the retreat of 1942 and the Fourteenth Army to victory at Imphal and Kohima in 1944 and the reconquest of Burma in 1945.

## Career

Born in Bristol. He commanded the 10th Indian Infantry Division in the Middle East, then Burma Corps in the 1942 retreat to India, XV Corps, and from 1943 the Fourteenth Army, which defeated the Japanese invasion of India in 1944 and retook Burma in 1945. After the war he was Chief of the Imperial General Staff and Governor-General of Australia (1953–60).

## Command style

Close to his soldiers and blunt with everyone. He insisted on medical care and malaria discipline, supplied encircled "boxes" by air instead of retreating, delegated decisions, and preferred to outflank and bypass Japanese positions, as in the armoured thrust to Meiktila in 1945.
