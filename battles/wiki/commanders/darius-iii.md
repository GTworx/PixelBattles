# Darius III

## Summary

Last king of the Achaemenid Empire (336–330 BCE). He commanded the centre at [[gaugamela]] and fled the field when [[alexander-the-great]]'s wedge reached him.

## Career

Lost to Alexander at Issus (333 BCE), where he also fled. He gathered an army from across the empire and chose the open plain at Gaugamela, having it levelled for [[scythed-chariots]] and cavalry. After defeat he escaped (Arrian: towards Media; TheCollector: to Ecbatana) and was seized and murdered by [[bessus]] in 330 BCE.

## Command style

His preparations at Gaugamela were sound: open ground, cavalry mass, chariots, a plan to outflank the Macedonians. Weaknesses were the unwieldy mixed army and his own position in the centre, where he was an obvious target; his flight at the critical moment broke Persian resistance.

## Battles

- [[gaugamela]] — commanded the centre.
