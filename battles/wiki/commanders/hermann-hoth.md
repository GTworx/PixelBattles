# Hermann Hoth

## Summary

Panzer general who led the 4th Panzer Army, which attacked Stalingrad from the south in 1942 and supported the relief attempt.

## Career

Commander of the 4th Panzer Army. On 23 August 1942 the 6th Army and 4th Panzer Army attacked Stalingrad. In December his army provided the LVII Panzer Corps for Operation Winter Storm, from Kotelnikovo.

## Command style

Armoured spearheads in open country: the corps in the city fighting was poorly suited, while the relief force was weak (the 23rd Panzer Division had about thirty serviceable tanks at first).
