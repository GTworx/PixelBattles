# Callimachus

## Summary

Athenian polemarch (war archon) in 490 BCE and nominal commander at [[marathon]]. With the ten strategoi split on whether to attack, his casting vote, urged by [[miltiades]], decided the battle.

## Career

Almost nothing is known beyond Marathon. By tradition the polemarch held the place of honour on the right wing, where the Athenians won, and Callimachus was killed there. Athens later honoured him with a monument on the Acropolis.

## Command style

Not recoverable from the sources; he is remembered chiefly for the vote and his death in the front line.
