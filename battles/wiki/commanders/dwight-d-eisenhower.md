# Dwight D. Eisenhower

## Summary

American general who commanded the Allied landings in North Africa in 1942 and, as Supreme Commander of the Allied Expeditionary Force, the invasion of Normandy and the campaign into Germany in 1944–45; later the 34th President of the United States.

## Career

Born in Denison, Texas. A staff officer between the wars, he joined the War Department's planning staff in 1941, took command of US forces in Europe in June 1942 and led Operation Torch in November 1942 and the Allied forces in the Mediterranean through Tunisia, Sicily and the invasion of Italy. Appointed to command SHAEF on 24 December 1943, he directed Overlord and the advance to the Elbe. After the war he was Army Chief of Staff, NATO's first Supreme Allied Commander Europe (1951–52) and President (1953–61).

## Command style

A coalition commander more than a battlefield tactician: he held together British, American, French and other forces and strong-willed subordinates such as Montgomery and Patton, gave them latitude, and took personal responsibility for the decision to launch D-Day on 6 June 1944.
