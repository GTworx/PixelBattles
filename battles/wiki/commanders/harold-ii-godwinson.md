# Harold II Godwinson

Last crowned Anglo-Saxon king of England, killed at [[hastings]].

## Summary

Son of Earl Godwin of Wessex, Harold was chosen by the Witan after Edward the Confessor's death in January 1066 and crowned on 6 January. He reigned about nine months and fought two invasions in three weeks.

## Career

He held the earldoms of East Anglia, Wessex (from 1053) and Hereford (from 1058) and led campaigns against Gruffudd ap Llywelyn of Wales in 1062-1063. In 1066 he watched the south coast against William, dismissed the levy in early September, then marched north and killed Harald Hardrada and his brother Tostig at Stamford Bridge on 25 September. He then marched about 240 miles south to face William.

## Command style

A fast-moving commander who relied on forced marches and quick decisions. At Hastings he took a defensive position on a ridge and fought with [[housecarls]] and fyrd in a [[shield-wall]]. Accounts of his death conflict (arrow, sword, or both), and whether he ordered the pursuit that broke his line is unclear.
