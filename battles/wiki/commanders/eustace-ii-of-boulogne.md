# Eustace II of Boulogne

Count of Boulogne who, with William FitzOsbern, led the right wing of the Norman army at [[hastings]].

## Summary

Eustace commanded French, Picard, Flemish and Boulonnais troops. At the Malfosse, in the closing action, he was seriously wounded by English fugitives who turned to fight. His later career is not covered by the sources consulted.
