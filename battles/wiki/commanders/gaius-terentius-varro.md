# Gaius Terentius Varro

## Summary

Roman consul in 216 BCE who held the command on the day of [[cannae]] and led the left wing. He escaped the battle.

## Career

A "new man" elected on a platform of vigorous action against [[hannibal-barca]]. He repelled a Carthaginian ambush before the battle, which raised Roman confidence, and on the battle day (Livy has him in command) deployed the army with a deep infantry centre. He fled to Venusia with a small cavalry escort (about 70 in Polybius, about 50 in Livy, who criticises the flight).

## Command style

Aggressive and confident. Livy makes him the villain of Cannae, though Polybius records that the consuls commanded on alternate days.
