# Bessus

## Summary

Satrap of Bactria who commanded the Persian left wing at [[gaugamela]], then murdered [[darius-iii]] and briefly claimed the throne.

## Career

Led the Bactrian, Scythian, Dahae and other eastern cavalry opposite [[alexander-the-great]]'s right. After the defeat he caught up with the fleeing Darius and, in 330 BCE, killed him and fled east, taking the throne name Artaxerxes V. Alexander pursued him through Bactria and Sogdiana; he was captured, tried and executed in 329 BCE.

## Command style

His horsemen made the hardest fight of the day against the Macedonian right, and he kept them together when Darius left the field. By shadowing Alexander's drift to the right he also helped to open the gap that decided the battle.

## Battles

- [[gaugamela]] — commanded the left.
