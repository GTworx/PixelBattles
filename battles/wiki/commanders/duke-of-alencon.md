# Jean I, Duke of Alençon

## Summary

French noble who led the main battle at Agincourt and was killed in the melee.

## Career

Commander of the second French line, with the Duke of Bar. Killed in the fighting.

## Command style

Aggressive, noble-led charge into the narrow field.
