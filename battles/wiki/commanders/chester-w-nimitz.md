# Chester W. Nimitz

## Summary

US admiral who commanded the Pacific Fleet and the Pacific Ocean Areas from December 1941 to the end of the war, directing Midway and the Central Pacific drive.

## Career

Born in Fredericksburg, Texas. A submariner and the US Navy's leading submarine authority between the wars, he was Chief of the Bureau of Navigation in 1939–41. He took over the Pacific Fleet after Pearl Harbor and from 1942 was Commander in Chief, Pacific Ocean Areas. He accepted Japan's surrender for the United States in Tokyo Bay and was Chief of Naval Operations in 1945–47.

## Command style

Calm, strategic and offensive-minded. He trusted his codebreakers and concentrated his few carriers at Midway on their warning, chose able subordinates (Spruance, Halsey) and, with Admiral King, pressed the Central Pacific route across the island chains against MacArthur's preference for New Guinea and the Philippines.
