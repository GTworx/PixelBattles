# Mehmed II "the Conqueror"

## Summary

Ottoman sultan who captured Constantinople in 1453 at age 21, ending the Byzantine Empire.

## Career

Became sultan in 1451. Built Rumeli Hisari in 1452 to control the Bosporus, assembled the army, a fleet and the great bombard of Orban, and pushed the siege through 53 days. He made Constantinople his capital.

## Command style

Adaptive and decisive: when the chain stopped his ships he had them dragged overland; he offered on 21 May to lift the siege in exchange for the city, and then staged the three-wave assault of 29 May, with irregulars first, regulars second and Janissaries last.
