# Douglas MacArthur

## Summary

American general who commanded in the Philippines in 1941–42, led the Allied South West Pacific Area from New Guinea back to the Philippines, and governed occupied Japan.

## Career

Born in Little Rock, Arkansas. Army Chief of Staff in 1930–35 and military adviser to the Philippines from 1935, he commanded US Army Forces in the Far East when Japan attacked. Ordered out of Corregidor in 1942, he became Supreme Commander of the South West Pacific Area, advanced along New Guinea and returned to the Philippines at Leyte in October 1944. He accepted Japan's surrender as Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, ran the occupation until 1951 and commanded UN forces in Korea until Truman removed him.

## Command style

He favoured "massive strokes against only main strategic objectives", bypassing strongpoints along the New Guinea coast, though he rejected the term "island hopping". He cultivated publicity and loyalty, often went forward himself, and was a controversial figure throughout.
