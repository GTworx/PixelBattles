# Napoleon Bonaparte

## Summary

Emperor of the French, one of history's great captains, whose last campaign ended at Waterloo.

## Career

Emperor of the French; abdicated in 1814, exiled to Elba, and returned in March 1815 (the Hundred Days). He beat the Prussians at Ligny on 16 June 1815 and fought Wellington at Waterloo two days later. He abdicated again on 22 June, was exiled to Saint Helena and died there in 1821.

## Command style

Seeks to defeat enemy armies separately by speed and central position, and in battle prepares an assault with massed artillery, then infantry and cavalry. At Waterloo he waited for the ground to dry, delegated much tactical control to Marshal [[michel-ney]], and detached [[emmanuel-de-grouchy]] with a third of his army after Ligny. When Prussian columns appeared he diverted the Young and Old Guard to Plancenoit and then committed the [[imperial-guard]] in a last attempt on Wellington's centre.
