# William the Conqueror (Duke William II of Normandy)

Duke of Normandy who won the crown of England at [[hastings]].

## Summary

Born at Falaise, illegitimate son of Duke Robert I and Herleva, William became duke as a child in 1035. He was crowned king of England at Westminster on Christmas Day 1066.

## Career

He survived years of noble infighting, secured his rule after Val-es-Dunes (1047) with help from Henry I of France, subdued rivals in the 1050s, married Matilda of Flanders and secured Maine by about 1064. After Edward's death he claimed the throne on the strength of a promise by Edward and an oath by Harold, assembled a fleet and landed at Pevensey on 28 September 1066. After Hastings he faced revolts and conducted the Harrying of the North (1069-1070). He ordered the Domesday survey in 1086 and died campaigning in France.

## Command style

A formidable fighter and horseman who combined archers, infantry and [[heavy-cavalry]]. At Hastings he rallied his army when a rumour of his death spread by riding bareheaded along the line, and he used [[feigned-retreat]]s to draw the English off their ridge. He relied on castles to hold territory.
