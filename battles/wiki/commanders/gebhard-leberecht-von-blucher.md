# Gebhard Leberecht von Blücher

## Summary

Prussian field marshal, aged 72 in 1815, whose army's arrival at Waterloo decided the day.

## Career

Beaten at Ligny on 16 June 1815, he withdrew to Wavre, not eastward, and assured Wellington of support on 18 June. He marched with Bülow's IV Corps, followed by Zieten's I and Pirch's II Corps; with Gneisenau as chief of staff he kept the columns moving through mud and defiles.

## Command style

Resolute: he kept the Prussian columns marching from Wavre despite delay and mud. Bülow reached Plancenoit at about 16:30; Zieten's corps reached Wellington's left after about 17:30. Blücher met Wellington near La Belle Alliance and the Prussians led the pursuit.
