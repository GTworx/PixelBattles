# Nikephoros Bryennios

Commander of the Byzantine left wing at [[manzikert]].

## Summary

On 24 August a foraging force under Bryennios met the main Seljuk army and was nearly encircled; World History Encyclopedia says he was wounded three times, though not seriously. On 26 August he led the left wing, which broke in the Seljuk counter-attack. Further biography is not covered by the pages consulted.
