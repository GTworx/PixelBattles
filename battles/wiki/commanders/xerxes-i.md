# Xerxes I

## Summary

King of Persia 486-465 BCE who led the great invasion of Greece in 480 BCE and commanded personally at [[thermopylae]].

## Career

Succeeding [[darius-i]], he suppressed revolts in Egypt and Babylon, then bridged the Hellespont and dug the Athos canal. After forcing Thermopylae he burned Athens, lost the fleet at Salamis, and returned to Asia leaving Mardonius, who lost at Plataea (479). He was assassinated in 465.

## Command style

A king directing a multi-national army from a throne above the field; at Thermopylae he waited four days, rotated contingents in frontal assaults, then exploited the traitor's path.
