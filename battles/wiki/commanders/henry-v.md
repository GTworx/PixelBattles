# Henry V of England

## Summary

King of England from 1413, who renewed the Hundred Years' War and won the victory of Agincourt against heavy odds.

## Career

Claimed the French throne through Edward III. Landed in Normandy in August 1415, took Harfleur on 22 September after a costly siege, and marched toward Calais with about 9,000 men. After Agincourt he conquered Normandy and, by the Treaty of Troyes (1420), became regent and heir of Charles VI. He died of illness in 1422, before he could inherit.

## Command style

Personally led from the centre at Agincourt; adapted when the French refused to attack by advancing to a closer position and re-planting the stakes. He fought in the melee, protected his brother Humphrey of Gloucester, and ordered the killing of most prisoners when he feared a French counter-attack, a decision still debated.
