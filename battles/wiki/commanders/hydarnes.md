# Hydarnes

## Summary

Persian general commanding the [[immortals]] at [[thermopylae]], who led the night march up the Anopaea path that took the Greek position from behind.

## Career

Son of a Hydarnes who helped Darius take the throne. In 480 Herodotus has him lead the Immortals on the mountain path under the guidance of [[ephialtes-of-trachis]]. Diodorus gives the flanking force as 20,000.

## Command style

Known mainly for the single manoeuvre at Thermopylae.
