# Leofwine Godwinson

Brother of [[harold-ii-godwinson]]; killed at [[hastings]].

## Summary

Leofwine fought with the king and died at Hastings together with [[gyrth-godwinson]], possibly while leading the pursuit of the Bretons. The sources consulted mention him only as one of the brothers killed, so biographical details are not established here.
