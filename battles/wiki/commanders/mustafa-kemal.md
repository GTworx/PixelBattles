# Mustafa Kemal (Atatürk)

## Summary

Ottoman officer who made his name holding the heights above Anzac Cove at Gallipoli in 1915, and later founded the Republic of Turkey.

## Career

An Ottoman staff officer who had fought in Libya and the Balkan Wars, he commanded the 19th Division in Liman von Sanders's 5th Army on the Gallipoli peninsula. On 25 April 1915 he counter-attacked the ANZAC landing on his own initiative and held the Sari Bair ridge, ordering his 57th Regiment: "I do not order you to attack, I order you to die"; in August he again held Chunuk Bair and Anafarta against the Suvla offensive. He later commanded the 16th Corps in the Caucasus (retaking Bitlis and Muş in 1916) and the 7th Army in Palestine, which he withdrew north after Megiddo in 1918. He led the Turkish War of Independence (1919–1923) and became the first President of Turkey.

## Command style

Decisive and quick to act on his own judgement at the critical point, with a sure eye for ground.
