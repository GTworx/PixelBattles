# Jean II Le Maingre, "Boucicaut"

## Summary

Marshal of France, taken prisoner at Agincourt.

## Career

Commanded the vanguard alongside d'Albret. Like the Constable he favoured a patient approach, preferring to starve the English, but was overruled; he was captured at Agincourt.

## Command style

A seasoned, cautious commander; overruled at Agincourt.
