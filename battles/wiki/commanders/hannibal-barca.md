# Hannibal Barca

## Summary

Carthaginian general who invaded Italy in the Second Punic War and destroyed a Roman army at [[cannae]] in 216 BCE.

## Career

He crossed the Alps in 218 BCE and beat the Romans at the Trebia and Lake Trasimene before Cannae. After Cannae, Rome refused terms and the war continued another 14 years, ending with his defeat at Zama in 202 BCE. Biographical details beyond the Cannae sources are not covered here.

## Command style

A master of deception, terrain and the combined use of different troop types. At Cannae he used his weaker troops as a flexible centre, kept his best infantry in reserve at the wing tips, and used superior cavalry to close the net behind the Romans: the classic [[double-envelopment]]. He chose his ground to put sun and wind against the enemy. After the victory he declined to march on Rome, a decision ancient and modern writers dispute.
