# Romanos IV Diogenes

Byzantine emperor 1068-1071, captured at [[manzikert]].

## Summary

A Cappadocian general with a frontier reputation in Syria and on the Danube, crowned on 1 January 1068 after marrying the dowager empress Eudokia Makrembolitissa.

## Career

He campaigned against the Seljuks in 1068-1069 without a decisive success and alienated the nobility, mercenaries and the Doukas family with unpopular reforms. At Manzikert he divided his army, rejected a Seljuk peace offer and was wounded and captured. After about eight days he was released on a treaty and ransom, was defeated by the Doukai in Cilicia, surrendered at Adana and was blinded at Kotyaion on 29 June 1072. He died on Prote of an infected wound.

## Command style

Described as harsh and violent in judgement; he repeatedly exposed himself to danger, which worried his generals. Historians argue his failure to address the army's weaknesses was the chief reason for the defeat.
