# Michel Ney

## Summary

Marshal of France, "the bravest of the brave", who directed most of the French tactical fighting at Waterloo.

## Career

Marshal of the Empire. In 1815 he led the attack at Quatre Bras on 16 June, which ended in a draw (about 4,000 losses a side). At Waterloo he exercised tactical control of most French forces for most of the battle.

## Command style

Courageous and personally forward, but impetuous. At about 16:00 he took Allied wounded and baggage moving to the rear for a retreat and launched the cavalry reserve against the ridge (about 9,000 horse in all), and without infantry or guns it could not break the [[infantry-square]]s. He later led attacks that took La Haye Sainte.
