# Isoroku Yamamoto

## Summary

Commander-in-chief of Japan's Combined Fleet from 1939 to 1943, who planned the attack on Pearl Harbor and the Midway operation.

## Career

Born in Nagaoka, Niigata. He commanded the carrier Akagi and the First Carrier Division, was Vice Minister of the Navy in 1936–39 and took command of the Combined Fleet in August 1939. He planned the Pearl Harbor strike, threatening to resign to win its approval, and the Midway operation that cost Japan four carriers. After the Guadalcanal campaign, on 18 April 1943, American fighters, acting on decoded signals, shot down his aircraft over Bougainville.

## Command style

Aggressive and innovative: he concentrated six carriers in one striking force and pushed for long-range land-based bombers, seeking a quick decisive blow because he believed Japan could not win a long war with the United States. His Midway plan was complex and hastily prepared, and after it he was drawn into the attrition he had feared.
