# Alan the Red

Breton nobleman who led the left wing of the Norman army at [[hastings]].

## Summary

Alan commanded the left division of Bretons, Angevins, Poitevins and Maine men. That division broke and fled during the early assaults, starting the rumour that William was dead and drawing part of the English line off the ridge. His later career is not covered by the sources consulted.
