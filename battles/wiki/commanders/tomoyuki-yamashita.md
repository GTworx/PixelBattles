# Tomoyuki Yamashita

## Summary

Japanese general, "the Tiger of Malaya", who conquered Malaya and Singapore in 1941–42 and defended the Philippines in 1944–45.

## Career

Born in Otoyo, Kochi. He commanded the 25th Army in the Malayan campaign, taking Singapore on 15 February 1942 with about 80,000 prisoners, then the 1st Area Army in Manchukuo. In October 1944 he took over the 14th Area Army in the Philippines and fought on in northern Luzon until the surrender. Navy troops who defied his orders to leave Manila massacred civilians there in February 1945. He was tried by an American military tribunal for failing to prevent his troops' atrocities and hanged in 1946; the "Yamashita standard" of command responsibility comes from his case.

## Command style

Fast and audacious in Malaya, where a smaller army outmanoeuvred a larger one; in Luzon, a stubborn defence in the mountains with forces he could not fully control.
