# Vasily Chuikov

## Summary

Commander of the 62nd Army, which held the city of Stalingrad from 11-12 September 1942.

## Career

Soviet officer who commanded the 62nd Army at Stalingrad from September 1942 (sources give 11–12 September; one source dates his command from mid-August) to April 1943.

## Command style

Held the front line close to the Germans ("hugging" tactics) so the Luftwaffe could not strike without hitting its own men, relied on small assault groups, strongpoints and Volga-bank artillery, and kept command bunkers near the front. See [[urban-warfare]].
