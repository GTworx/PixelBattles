# Mazaeus

## Summary

Persian satrap who commanded the Persian right wing at [[gaugamela]] (his later career is outside the sources consulted here).

## Career

Fought at Gaugamela with Syrian, Median, Mesopotamian, Parthian and other cavalry, leading the attack on [[parmenion]]'s left (Warfare History Network says he sent horsemen round the flank towards the baggage). After the battle he withdrew with his cavalry; Wikipedia's Gaugamela article credits Bagophanes, not Mazaeus, with surrendering Babylon.

## Command style

His cavalry came closest to success: they pressed the Macedonian left hard and some broke through to the camp. When [[darius-iii]] fled, he disengaged and withdrew in good order.

## Battles

- [[gaugamela]] — commanded the right.
