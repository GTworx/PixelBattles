# Enver Pasha

## Summary

Young Turk leader and Ottoman Minister of War, who took the empire into the war on Germany's side and led the 3rd Army to disaster at Sarikamish.

## Career

A leader of the Committee of Union and Progress in the 1908 revolution, he fought the Italians in Libya (1911–12) and retook Edirne in the Second Balkan War. As one of the "Three Pashas" and Minister of War from 1914 he signed the secret alliance with Germany. In December 1914 he took personal command of the 3rd Army for a winter flanking march over the mountains to encircle the Russians at Sarikamish; cold, hunger and the Russian counter-attack destroyed most of the army (estimates of the losses run from 60,000 to over 90,000). He shared responsibility for the Armenian genocide of 1915–16. In 1918 he sent the Army of Islam to Baku. He fled after the Armistice of Mudros and was killed fighting the Red Army in Central Asia in 1922.

## Command style

Bold, ambitious and an admirer of German methods, but his plans ignored terrain, weather and supply.
