# Hasdrubal (cavalry commander at Cannae)

## Summary

Carthaginian officer who led the Iberian and Gallic heavy cavalry on [[hannibal-barca]]'s left at [[cannae]]. He is usually distinguished from Hannibal's brother Hasdrubal Barca; sources and modern writers differ on his full name and identity, so treat that as uncertain.

## Career

Little is known beyond Cannae. Polybius credits him with commanding about 6,000–7,000 Iberian and Gallic horsemen on the river flank and with the manoeuvre that decided the battle.

## Command style

After routing the Roman right-wing cavalry on the Aufidus, he took his squadrons behind the entire Roman army to the other wing, helped the Numidians scatter the allied cavalry there, and then struck the Roman rear. That disciplined, flexible handling of cavalry completed the encirclement.

## Battles

- [[cannae]] — commanded the left-wing cavalry.
