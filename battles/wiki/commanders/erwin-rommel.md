# Erwin Rommel

## Summary

German field marshal, "the Desert Fox", who led the 7th Panzer Division in France in 1940, the Afrika Korps and Panzer Army Africa in North Africa in 1941–43, and Army Group B in Normandy in 1944.

## Career

Born in Heidenheim. In 1940 his 7th Panzer Division raced through France. Sent to Libya in February 1941, he retook Cyrenaica, besieged Tobruk, took it in June 1942 after Gazala and reached El Alamein, where he was stopped in July and defeated in October–November 1942. He commanded Army Group Africa briefly in 1943, then served in Italy and inspected the western defences before taking Army Group B in Normandy. Wounded on 17 July 1944, he was implicated in the plot against Hitler and forced to kill himself on 14 October 1944.

## Command style

Fast, mobile and opportunistic: surprise, speed and leading from the front, often against superior numbers. Brusque with superiors and at times willing to disobey Hitler; his supply situation was the weak point of his desert campaigns.
