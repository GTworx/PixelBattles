# Leonidas I

## Summary

Agiad king of Sparta who commanded the allied Greek force at [[thermopylae]] and died there, covering the retreat of most of the army.

## Career

He became king about 489 BCE. In 480 he marched north with 300 Spartan hoplites (men with living sons), picking up allies on the way, to hold the pass for the Hellenic League. His reply to a surrender demand is recorded as "Molon labe" ("come and take them").

## Command style

Used the narrow ground and relays of contingents, fought in rotation, and on the third day chose to dismiss most allies and make a rearguard stand rather than lose the whole army.

## Battles

[[thermopylae]]; see also [[hoplite-phalanx]].
