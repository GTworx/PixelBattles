# Hayreddin Barbarossa (Hızır Reis)

## Summary

Corsair from Lesbos who became Kapudan Pasha (grand admiral) of the Ottoman navy and won the Battle of Preveza in 1538, the victory that made the Ottomans the leading naval power in the Mediterranean.

## Career

Born Hızır on Lesbos, he and his brother Oruç took Algiers from the Spanish sphere in 1516; on Oruç's death in 1518 Hızır inherited his nickname "Barbarossa" and became ruler of Algiers under Ottoman suzerainty. He took the Peñón of Algiers from Spain in 1529, and in 1533 Suleiman the Magnificent made him Kapudan Pasha. He took Tunis in 1534 but lost it to Charles V's expedition in 1535. In 1537 he raided Apulia, took a string of Venetian islands and besieged Corfu. In 1538 he defeated the Holy League off Preveza. In the 1540s he campaigned jointly with the French, including against Nice, and he retired to Constantinople in 1545, dying there in 1546.

## Command style

A corsair turned fleet commander. At Preveza he used the shelter of an Ottoman gulf and its fortress to refuse battle on the enemy's terms, came out only when the Holy League divided itself, kept close to his own shore, attacked the becalmed sailing ships with galleys that did not need the wind, and would not take Doria's bait.
