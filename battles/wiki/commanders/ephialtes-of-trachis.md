# Ephialtes of Trachis

## Summary

Local shepherd, son of Eurydemos, who for a reward showed [[xerxes-i]]'s army the Anopaea mountain track around [[thermopylae]]. His name became the Greek word for traitor.

## Career

After Salamis he fled to Thessaly, with a price on his head put by the Amphictyons. Around 470 BCE Athenades of Trachis killed him; Herodotus says the motive had nothing to do with the betrayal, but the Spartans rewarded Athenades anyway. Livius doubts the whole story, since Persian scouts could have found the path unaided.

## Command style

Not a commander; he guided [[hydarnes]] and the Immortals by night.
