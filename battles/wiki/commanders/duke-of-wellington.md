# Arthur Wellesley, Duke of Wellington

## Summary

British field marshal who led the Anglo-allied army at Waterloo and later became Prime Minister.

## Career

In 1815 he commanded the Anglo-allied (Dutch-Belgian, German and British) army in Belgium; held Napoleon's left at Quatre Bras on 16 June and chose the Mont-Saint-Jean position, giving battle on 18 June after Blücher promised support. Later Prime Minister (1828-30).

## Command style

Defensive, economical and cautious with his men: positions behind a crest, strongpoints forward, [[reverse-slope-defence]], infantry in [[infantry-square]]s against cavalry, and a hold on his reserves. At Waterloo he rode the line all day, and on seeing the Guard recoil at about 19:30 signalled a general advance. He called the day "the nearest-run thing you ever saw in your life".
