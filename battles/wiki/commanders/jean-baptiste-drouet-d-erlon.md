# Jean-Baptiste Drouet, comte d'Erlon

## Summary

Commander of I Corps (about 16,000 infantry), whose attack opened the main French assault at Waterloo.

## Career

Napoleonic corps commander. In 1815 he led I Corps (about 16,000 infantry). At Waterloo he held the French right, east of the Brussels road.

## Command style

At about 13:30 his four divisions advanced in dense columns on Wellington's left-centre. They were stopped by Allied infantry and driven back by the Household and Union cavalry charge, losing about 3,000 men; the corps needed until about 16:00 to re-form, and part of it later took La Haye Sainte. His men broke again when Zieten's Prussians struck the French right.
