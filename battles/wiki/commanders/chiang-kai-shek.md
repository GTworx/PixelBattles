# Chiang Kai-shek

## Summary

Leader of Nationalist China and commander-in-chief of its armies throughout the war with Japan from 1937 to 1945.

## Career

Born in Xikou, Zhejiang. Commandant of the Whampoa Military Academy and commander of the National Revolutionary Army, he led the Northern Expedition (1926–28) that unified China under the Kuomintang. From 1937 he fought Japan at Shanghai and Nanjing, then moved the government to Wuhan and Chongqing, holding out through eight years of war; he attended the Cairo Conference in 1943. After losing the Civil War he led the Republic of China on Taiwan until his death.

## Command style

He kept final authority over major decisions and played subordinates and factions against one another. He long put suppressing the Communists before resisting Japan, and accepted enormous losses, including scorched-earth measures that cost many civilian lives. His relations with his American chief of staff, Joseph Stilwell, broke down in 1944.
