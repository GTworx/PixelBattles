# Odo of Bayeux

Bishop of Bayeux and half-brother of [[william-the-conqueror]] through their mother Herleva.

## Summary

Bishop from 1049, Odo is one of the few men proven to have been with William at [[hastings]]. The Bayeux Tapestry shows him with a club rallying the troops, captioned as giving strength to the young men. Some historians read this as encouragement rather than killing; clubs were common among leaders.

## Career

Earl of Kent from 1067 and a trusted minister, sometimes regent in William's absence. He likely commissioned the Bayeux Tapestry. He was tried for fraud at Penenden Heath in 1076, imprisoned from 1082 until William's deathbed release in 1087, backed Robert Curthose's claim, and died at Palermo in 1097 on the way to the First Crusade.

## Command style

A warrior-prelate who led by example and exhortation.
