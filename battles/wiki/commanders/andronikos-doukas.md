# Andronikos Doukas

Byzantine general, first cousin of Michael VII and son of Caesar John Doukas; commanded the rearguard at [[manzikert]].

## Summary

He led the rearguard, announced that the emperor had been "cut down" and left the field, and was widely blamed for the defeat and Romanos's capture. Whether this was treachery, panic or misunderstanding is disputed.

## Career

After Romanos was released, Andronikos and his brother Constantine defeated him in Cilicia and Andronikos obtained his surrender, reportedly opposing the blinding. He became megas domestikos. In 1074 he and his father were beaten and captured by Roussel de Bailleul's mercenaries at the Zompos Bridge. He died of edema in October 1077.

## Command style

Not described in the sources consulted; Manzikert is the defining episode.
