# Erich von Manstein

## Summary

Field marshal who commanded Army Group Don and tried to relieve the Stalingrad pocket.

## Career

Commander of Army Group Don. He launched Operation Winter Storm from Kotelnikovo (12 December) with LVII Panzer Corps and Hoth's 4th Panzer Army; it got within about 48 km before Paulus declined to break out and Manstein abandoned it on 23 December.

## Command style

Operational, mobile-warfare doctrine; he pressed Hitler for freedom of action but could not give the relief force enough strength.
