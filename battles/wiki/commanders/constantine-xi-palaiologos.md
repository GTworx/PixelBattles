# Constantine XI Palaiologos

## Summary

The last Byzantine emperor, who died defending Constantinople.

## Career

Accepted church union in 1452 in hope of help; refused Mehmed's offer of terms on 21 May. He took post at the Mesoteichion. The manner of his death is uncertain: accounts have him charging in the street near the St Romanus Gate, hanging himself (Barbaro), or dying in a chance encounter with marines.

## Command style

Led in person, kept the Latin and Greek allies together, and held the most threatened sector.
