# Bernard Montgomery

## Summary

British field marshal who commanded the Eighth Army from El Alamein to Tunisia, Sicily and Italy, and the Allied ground forces in Normandy and then the 21st Army Group to the end of the war in Europe.

## Career

Born in Kennington, London. He commanded the 3rd Infantry Division in France in 1939–40, then corps and South-Eastern Command in England. Given the Eighth Army in August 1942, he won at Alam el Halfa and the Second Battle of El Alamein (October–November 1942) and pursued the Axis into Tunisia, then led the Eighth Army in Sicily and Italy. He commanded all Allied ground forces in Normandy until 1 September 1944 and the 21st Army Group thereafter, including Operation Market Garden. After the war he was Chief of the Imperial General Staff and Deputy Supreme Commander of NATO.

## Command style

Methodical: thorough preparation, a large material superiority and set-piece battles fought only when he judged them decisive, with frequent visits to the troops. Critics thought him slow and argued over his Normandy plan; at Alamein he refused any plan for retreat.
