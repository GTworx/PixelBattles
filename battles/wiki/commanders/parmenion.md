# Parmenion

## Summary

Philip II's senior general and Alexander's second-in-command, who held the Macedonian left at [[gaugamela]] against [[mazaeus]]'s cavalry.

## Career

Commander of the Macedonian left at Gaugamela. In Arrian he advised a night attack, which [[alexander-the-great]] refused. His later career and death are outside the sources consulted here.

## Command style

A steady, defensive wing commander. At Gaugamela his orders were to hold at all costs while Alexander won the battle on the right; when pressed by Persian cavalry and by raiders who broke through the line, he sent messengers asking Alexander for help. His troops later secured the Persian camp and baggage (TheCollector).

## Battles

- [[gaugamela]] — commanded the left.
