# Artaphernes

## Summary

Co-commander with [[datis]] of the Persian expedition to Greece in 490 BCE. He was the son of Artaphernes, satrap of Sardis, and nephew of [[darius-i]].

## Career

He shared command of the fleet and army that sacked Eretria and landed at [[marathon]]. After the defeat he withdrew with the survivors to Asia. In the 480 invasion a man of the same name, probably the same, led the Lydians and Mysians for Xerxes.

## Command style

Little is recorded of him individually; Herodotus treats the two commanders as a pair.
