# Heinz Guderian

## Summary

German general, an architect of the panzer arm, who led the armoured corps that crossed the Meuse at Sedan and reached the Channel in 1940 and Panzer Group 2 in the invasion of the Soviet Union.

## Career

Born in Kulm, West Prussia. He commanded XIX Corps in Poland and in France, where it broke through at Sedan on 13 May 1940. In Barbarossa his panzer group drove on Smolensk and was turned south to close the Kiev pocket; he was relieved on 25 December 1941 after retreating before Moscow. He returned as Inspector General of Armoured Troops in 1943 and was acting Chief of the General Staff from July 1944 to March 1945.

## Command style

"Leading from the front" in a radio-equipped command vehicle, concentrating armour at the decisive point and pushing on regardless of orders to halt. His memoirs overstate his own role in creating the panzer arm.
