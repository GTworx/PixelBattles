# Emmanuel de Grouchy

## Summary

Marshal of the Empire whose 33,000-man wing was at Wavre, not Waterloo, on 18 June 1815.

## Career

After Ligny, Napoleon detached him with 33,000 men at about 11:30 on 17 June to pursue the Prussians. On the morning of 18 June he was ordered to continue on Wavre; at about 13:15 Napoleon, seeing Prussian columns near Lasne, sent word to close with the main army, but Grouchy was too far away. He fought Thielmann's Prussian III Corps at Wavre on 18-19 June and then retreated.

## Command style

Methodical, tied to his orders; he did not march toward the sound of the guns at Waterloo. His role in the defeat is long debated.
