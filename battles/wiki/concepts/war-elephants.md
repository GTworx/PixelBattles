# War elephants

## Definition

Elephants trained to carry troops or charge in battle, used by Indian armies and adopted by Persians and later Hellenistic and Carthaginian forces.

## How it worked

They were shock weapons against infantry and cavalry (horses fear them), but could panic and trample their own side. This page covers only what the consulted sources say about Gaugamela.

## Where it appears

- [[gaugamela]]: Arrian counts about 15 Indian elephants in [[darius-iii]]'s army. World History Encyclopedia and Warfare History Network say they were not used in the battle; TheCollector says they stayed in the Persian camp and that Parmenion later captured nearly a dozen. One popular summary has them committed with the chariots, which conflicts with the others and is treated here as unreliable. Warfare History Network adds that Alexander met war elephants in earnest in north-west India about five years later.
