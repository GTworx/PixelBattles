# Housecarls

## Definition

Paid, full-time household soldiers (Old Norse husk-arl, "house man") introduced to England by Svein Forkbeard and Cnut.

## How it worked

Armed in mail and conical helmets, many with two-handed long axes, they were better equipped and trained than the militia. They were fewer than the fyrd but formed the core of the army around the king and likely held the front ranks on the flanks.

## Where it appears

- [[hastings]]: The backbone of Harold's army, standing around the royal standard, thinned by the Norman feigned retreats and fighting on around his body after he fell.
