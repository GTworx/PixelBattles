# Napoleonic artillery

## Definition

Smoothbore muzzle-loading guns and howitzers firing round shot, canister and shell, grouped in batteries; at Waterloo the French massed about 80 guns in a "grande batterie".

## How it worked

Heavy bombardment was meant to soften the enemy line before infantry advanced; it was less effective at Waterloo because Wellington's troops sat behind the slopes. Howitzers shelled Hougoumont until it burned. At Waterloo the French fielded about 246-254 guns, the Allies 156, and the Prussians 126.

## Where it appears

- [[waterloo]]: An 80-gun grande batterie opened on Wellington's left-centre before d'Erlon's attack; howitzers burned Hougoumont; after La Haye Sainte fell, horse artillery fired canister into the Allied squares. See [[infantry-square]] and [[reverse-slope-defence]].
