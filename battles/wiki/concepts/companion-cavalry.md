# Companion cavalry

## Definition

The Macedonian aristocratic heavy cavalry (hetairoi), the Macedonian heavy cavalry that [[alexander-the-great]] led on the right wing at Gaugamela. Their strength (around 2,000 in the scenario) is an estimate, not taken from the consulted sources.

## How it worked

Deployed on the right wing in a wedge or rhomboid formation, they were the hammer to the [[macedonian-phalanx]]'s anvil: a charge at the point where the enemy line was thinnest or the leader was exposed.

## Where it appears

- [[gaugamela]]: the Companions made the wedge that drove through the gap in the Persian line at [[darius-iii]], then rode back to fight Parthian, Indian and Persian cavalry, losing about sixty men in the hardest fighting of the day.
