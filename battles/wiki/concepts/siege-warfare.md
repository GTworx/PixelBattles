# Siege warfare

## Definition

The art of attacking and defending fortified places: blockade, bombardment, mining, assault and relief.

## How it worked

At Constantinople: blockade by land and sea, bombardment, ships hauled overland to bypass a harbour chain, mining and counter-mining, an Ottoman siege tower built and destroyed, and a night assault in waves designed to exhaust defenders before the best troops attacked. The defenders' chain, nightly repairs and tunnel interception delayed but could not defeat an enemy with greater numbers and supplies.
