# Kamikaze

## Definition

Japanese suicide attacks, mostly by aircraft deliberately crashed into Allied ships, from October 1944 to the end of the war.

## How it worked

Short of trained pilots and aircraft able to survive American fighters, Japan organised Special Attack units whose pilots flew explosive-laden planes into ships. About 3,800 pilots died; the attacks killed more than 7,000 Allied naval personnel and sank several dozen warships (estimates range from about 34 to 57), and roughly one attack in five hit or nearly hit its target. The largest campaign came off Okinawa from April 1945.

## Where it appears

- [[pacific-war]]: first organised attacks at Leyte Gulf (25 October 1944, when the escort carrier St. Lo was sunk); the Kikusui mass attacks at Okinawa in April–June 1945.
