# Defensive terrain and funnelling

## Definition

Choosing ground that forces a larger enemy onto a narrow front and denies flank manoeuvre.

## How it worked

Henry chose a roughly 750-900 m defile between woods; the woods blocked outflanking and squeezed the French vanguard together, while rain-soaked ploughed ground slowed the charge.

## Where it appears

- [[agincourt]]: The woods of Tramecourt and Azincourt and the muddy field channelled the French attack.
