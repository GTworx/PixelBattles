# Urban warfare

## Definition

Combat in towns and cities, where buildings, rubble and cellars restrict movement, limit visibility and neutralize long-range fire and armour.

## How it worked

In Stalingrad the fighting went building to building, in the largest and deadliest urban battle in military history. Strongpoints such as the grain elevator, Pavlov's House (held by a platoon for about two months) and the factory halls into fortresses; Mamayev Kurgan changed hands repeatedly. The east-bank artillery supported the defenders.

## Where it appears

- [[stalingrad]]: 13 September to November 1942, in the city and factory district; see [[vasily-chuikov]] and [[friedrich-paulus]]. See also [[combined-arms]].
