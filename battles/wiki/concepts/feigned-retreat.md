# Feigned retreat

## Definition

A tactic in which troops pretend to flee to draw the enemy out of formation, then turn on them.

## How it worked

At Hastings, William of Poitiers says the Normans used it twice; some historians doubt it, though most accept it. English troops who followed apparently fleeing units down the slope lost the protection of the line and were cut down. At Manzikert the Seljuk horse archers repeatedly gave ground rather than fight.

## Where it appears

- [[hastings]]: The Norman feigned flights thinned the housecarls and drew out levies.
- [[manzikert]]: Seljuk horse archers repeatedly gave ground all afternoon to draw the Byzantine line apart before closing in at dusk.
