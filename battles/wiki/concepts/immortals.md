# The Immortals

## Definition

The elite Persian infantry corps of 10,000, so called because any fallen member was at once replaced so that the strength never dropped.

## How it worked

Armed with spear and bow behind wicker shields, and richly equipped, they formed the core of the king's army. At [[thermopylae]] they made the second assault on Day 1 and failed against the [[hoplite-phalanx]]; on the night of Day 2, led by [[hydarnes]] and guided by [[ephialtes-of-trachis]], they took the mountain path and came down behind the Greeks.

## Where it appears

- [[thermopylae]]

- [[gaugamela]]: the Immortals, about 10,000 foot guards, stood in the Persian left-centre beside [[darius-iii]]. They broke when the Companion wedge and phalanx drove at the king and Darius fled.
