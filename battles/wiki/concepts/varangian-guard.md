# Varangian Guard

## Definition

An elite unit of the Byzantine army from about the 10th to the 14th century, serving as the emperors' personal bodyguard.

## How it worked

Established formally by Basil II in 988 after Vladimir I of Kiev sent 6,000 men, it was first Norse and Rus' and, after the Norman Conquest, increasingly Anglo-Saxon; by Alexios I it was largely English.

## Where it appears

- [[manzikert]]: At Manzikert, virtually all the emperor's guards fell around him. After Hastings (see [[hastings]]) dispossessed English fighters joined its ranks.
