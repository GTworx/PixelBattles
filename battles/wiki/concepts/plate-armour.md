# Plate armour

## Definition

Articulated steel armour covering the whole body, standard for knights by the early 15th century.

## How it worked

Good protection against arrows and blades at normal ranges, but heavy (about 25-30 kg) and exhausting over mud; fallen wearers struggled to rise, and some were said to have drowned in their helmets.

## Where it appears

- [[agincourt]]: The weight of French armour in ploughed mud contributed to their destruction.
