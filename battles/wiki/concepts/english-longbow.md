# English longbow

## Definition

A tall self-bow of yew, drawn by trained archers to shoot heavy arrows at a high rate of fire.

## How it worked

Archers, about 80% of Henry V's army, massed on the flanks and shot volleys from about 250-300 yards. Stakes planted in the ground protected them from cavalry. Arrows killed and wounded horses, hit unarmoured faces and weak points, and wore down men-at-arms, who were forced to advance heads down. In the melee the archers dropped their bows and used swords, axes and mallets.
