# Janissaries

## Definition

The Ottoman sultan's elite standing infantry.

## How it worked

Numbering about 5,000-10,000 at Constantinople, they were Mehmed's final wave on 29 May. Ulubatli Hasan is said to have been first on the stockade.

## Where it appears

- [[constantinople]]: Third and decisive assault wave.
- [[preveza]]: About 3,000 Janissaries served aboard Barbarossa's fleet in 1538 (Turkish sources).
