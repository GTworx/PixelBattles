# Combined arms

## Definition

Using infantry, artillery, cavalry or armour, engineers and air power together so that each covers the other's weakness.

## How it worked

Napoleonic combined arms meant infantry in column, cavalry and guns in step; Ney's unsupported cavalry charges at Waterloo show the cost of breaking it. In 1942-43 the Red Army made its Uranus breakthrough with massed artillery, infantry and tank corps, and exploited with mobile groups; the German armour and air power lost much of their edge in the ruined city.

## Where it appears

- [[waterloo]]: the French cavalry went in without infantry or artillery support; the Allies combined squares, guns and counter-charging cavalry.
- [[stalingrad]]: [[operation-uranus]] pairs an artillery barrage with tank corps exploiting the breakthrough; [[urban-warfare]] splits combined arms into small assault groups.
