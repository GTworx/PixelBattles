# Horse archers

## Definition

Mounted bowmen who shoot from the saddle, skirmishing at range and avoiding close combat until the enemy is disordered.

## How it worked

At Manzikert the Seljuks withdrew in a crescent, loosing arrows at the flanks of the advancing Byzantines while keeping out of reach, then closed in when the Byzantine line turned back and lost cohesion.

## Where it appears

- [[manzikert]]: The core of Alp Arslan's army; they harassed the Byzantine advance all afternoon and ran down the broken wings at dusk.
