# Double envelopment

## Definition

A manoeuvre in which both wings of an army turn inward to attack the enemy's centre or rear, trapping it from two sides.

## How it worked

One side deliberately lets its centre give ground (or is weak), while its wings defeat the enemy's wings and then wheel inward. The enemy centre is pocketed with no room to fight or flee.

## Where it appears

- [[marathon]]: the Athenian wings beat the Persian levies, then wheeled in on the Persian centre while the retiring Athenian centre stood again. Whether this was planned by [[miltiades]] or improvised is debated.
- [[stalingrad]]: [[operation-uranus]] (19-23 Nov 1942) was a double envelopment on a vast scale: the Southwestern and Don Fronts from the north-west and the Stalingrad Front from the south smashed the Romanian flanks and met at Kalach, trapping the German 6th Army.

- [[cannae]]: [[hannibal-barca]] let his crescent centre give ground while the Libyan infantry wheeled in on both Roman flanks and [[hasdrubal]]'s cavalry struck the rear, trapping an army of some 80,000.
