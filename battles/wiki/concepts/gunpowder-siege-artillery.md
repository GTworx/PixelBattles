# Gunpowder siege artillery

## Definition

Cannon and bombards used to batter fortifications.

## How it worked

Orban's great bombard fired stone balls of around 270 kg, needed 60 oxen and hundreds of men to move, and was slow to reload. Together with ~70 other guns it battered the Mesoteichion; the defenders repaired by night with timber, earth and barrels. The guns did not alone win the siege but created the breach and wore down the garrison.

## Where it appears

- [[constantinople]]: The guns opened a breach at the Lycus valley and brought down sections of wall.
