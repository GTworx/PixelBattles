# Roman legion (manipular system)

## Definition

The Roman citizen legion of the Middle Republic: about 4,200–5,000 infantry and 300 horse, divided into velites (skirmishers), hastati, principes and triarii, in small tactical units (maniples) that normally deployed in a chequerboard (quincunx) with gaps between them.

## How it worked

Skirmishers fell back through the line, and the successive lines rotated into the fight, giving flexibility and depth. At Cannae the Romans departed from the loose chequerboard: they drew the maniples up narrower and deeper, with principes directly behind hastati, to force a breakthrough in the centre. That gave them weight but cost them frontage and the ability to manoeuvre.

## Where it appears

- [[cannae]]: eight legions with allied contingents; the deep centre was compressed into a column by [[hannibal-barca]]'s retiring crescent and destroyed by [[double-envelopment]].
