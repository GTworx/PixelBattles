# Defile defence

## Definition

Holding a narrow pass so that a numerically superior enemy can only attack on a front as wide as the pass.

## How it worked

The defender puts the best heavy infantry in the neck, adds a wall or fortification, and rotates units. The weakness is the flank: a track over the mountains negates the position, as at [[thermopylae]], where the Anopaea path let the [[immortals]] come down behind [[leonidas-i]]'s men.
