# Men-at-arms

## Definition

Armoured professional or noble heavy fighters, mounted or dismounted, the core of a medieval army.

## How it worked

At Agincourt both sides dismounted most of their men-at-arms. Henry's stood four deep in three battles; the French, ~10,000 strong, advanced on foot through mud. Packed too closely to swing weapons, many fell and were trampled or suffocated.

## Where it appears

- [[agincourt]]: English and French men-at-arms formed the centre lines.
