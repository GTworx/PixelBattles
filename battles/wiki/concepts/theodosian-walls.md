# Theodosian Walls

## Definition

The triple line of fortifications built in the fifth century across the western landward side of Constantinople, from the Golden Horn to the Sea of Marmara.

## How it worked

A moat over 20 m wide, a lower outer wall (about 8.5-9 m high) and a higher inner wall (about 12 m high, 96 towers). The walls dip into the Lycus valley (the Mesoteichion), the weakest section. The Blachernae walls at the north end were thicker but older. The defenders were too few to hold both lines and concentrated on the outer one.

## Where it appears

- [[constantinople]]: Held for roughly a thousand years until Ottoman guns and the numbers of 1453 wore them down.
