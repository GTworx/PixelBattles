# Numidian cavalry

## Definition

Light horsemen from North Africa, riding small horses without saddle or bridle, armed with javelins, and renowned for speed, harassment and pursuit.

## How it worked

They screened, skirmished and pursued. Carthage used them on its wings with the heavier Iberian and Gallic cavalry, which did the shock work. They were quick to exploit a broken enemy and to pursue fugitives.

## Where it appears

- [[cannae]]: about 3,500–4,000 Numidians under Hanno on the right wing pinned and then helped to rout the Roman allied cavalry, and chased down the fugitives afterwards.
