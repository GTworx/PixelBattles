# Operation Uranus

## Definition

The Soviet strategic counteroffensive of 19-23 November 1942 that encircled the German 6th Army at Stalingrad.

## How it worked

Stavka (with [[georgy-zhukov]] and Vasilevsky) massed fronts out of sight on the flanks of the 6th Army, which was held by weaker Romanian armies. On 19 November the Southwestern Front (1st Guards, 5th Tank and 21st Armies) and the Don Front attacked the Romanian Third Army, which was overrun; on 20 November the Stalingrad Front (51st and 57th Armies with the 4th and 13th Mechanized Corps) struck the Romanian Fourth Army in the south. The pincers met at Kalach on 23 November, sealing the ring. (Operation Mars was launched at the same time against Army Group Centre.) Reserves were thin and Hitler ordered the 6th Army to stay.
