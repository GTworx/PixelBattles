# Hoplite

## Definition

A citizen-soldier of an ancient Greek city-state, armed with a long thrusting spear (dory), a short sword, a large round shield (hoplon), a bronze helmet, breastplate and greaves. Hoplites were the core of Greek armies and fought in the [[hoplite-phalanx]].

## How it worked

Hoplites were men wealthy enough to buy their own armour, so a city's army was its citizen body. Athens fielded about 9,000-10,000 at Marathon; Spartans were full-time professional hoplites trained from childhood.

## Where it appears

- [[marathon]]: the whole Athenian and Plataean army.
- [[thermopylae]]: Spartans, Thespians, Thebans and Peloponnesian allies made up almost all the Greek force.
