# Heavy cavalry

## Definition

Mounted, armoured shock troops who fought with lance and sword.

## How it worked

At Hastings the Norman knights could not break the English shield wall on the slope by direct charge but were decisive in running down pursuers on open ground and in executing the feigned retreats.

## Where it appears

- [[hastings]]: About a quarter of the Norman army; their charges failed uphill but destroyed the English who left the line.
- [[waterloo]]: Ney's ~9,000 French cuirassiers and lancers charged Wellington's [[infantry-square]]s without infantry or gun support and failed; earlier, Uxbridge's Household and Union Brigades broke d'Erlon's columns but, out of control, were destroyed in turn.
