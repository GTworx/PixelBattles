# Tagmata

## Definition

The professional, centrally paid regiments of the Byzantine army, as distinct from provincial theme levies.

## How it worked

The Byzantine core at Manzikert consisted of tagmata from the eastern and western provinces, together with provincial troops, border levies and mercenaries. The sources consulted describe the army's decline in discipline and reliance on mercenaries before the battle but give no detailed account of the tagmata themselves.

## Where it appears

- [[manzikert]]: The professional core of Romanos's army, largely destroyed by the dawn after the battle.
